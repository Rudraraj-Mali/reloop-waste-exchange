ReLoop enforces core database-level invariants directly in the DBMS engine rather than application code:

🔒 ACID Concurrency: WITH (UPDLOCK, ROWLOCK) prevents double-booking ⚡ AFTER UPDATE Trigger: trg_ExchangeRequests_Completed auto-awards +10 eco-points 📊 Dynamic SQL View: UserImpactDashboard computes CO₂ savings & weight diverted
