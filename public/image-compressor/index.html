Lossy vs. Lossless Compression

⚡ Lossy (JPEG, WebP)

Removes data the eye won't notice. Achieves 60–90% reduction. Best for photos and web images.

  • ✓ Huge savings
  • ✓ Visually great at 80%+
  • ✗ Minor quality trade-off

🔒 Lossless (PNG)

Preserves every pixel. 10–30% savings. Best for logos, UI, transparent images.

  • ✓ Zero quality loss
  • ✓ Perfect for text & edges
  • ✗ Larger files for photos

Why Compress Images?

Images account for over 50% of the average webpage's total bytes. Unoptimized images are the #1 cause of slow page loads and poor Core Web Vitals — which directly affect Google rankings.

A 5 MB hero image can add 5–8 seconds of load time on mobile. Compressed to 300 KB it loads in under a second. Studies show a 1-second delay reduces conversions by 7%. Smaller images also lower CDN costs and help users on slow connections.
